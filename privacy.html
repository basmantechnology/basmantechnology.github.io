Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

BasMan Technology (“we”, “us”) runs the job portal at basmantechnology.github.io. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how we look after it.

Information we collect

  • When you apply or register: your name, email address, phone number, job title, experience, skills, location, LinkedIn link (if given) and the resume file you upload.
  • When you log in: a login session so you stay signed in. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash.
  • Applications: which jobs you applied for and when.

How we use it

  • To share your profile and resume with employers and recruitment partners for jobs you apply for or that match your profile.
  • To email you about your account, applications and (if you turn them on) job alerts.
  • We do not sell your information.

“Send from my Gmail” (Google permission)

If you choose Send from my Gmail, Google asks you to allow this website to send email on your behalf (the Gmail “send” permission). We use it only to send the one application email you review and click Send for, from your own Gmail account, with your resume attached.

  • We cannot read, search, delete or change any of your emails.
  • The permission is used in your browser only and is not stored on our servers. It expires automatically (about 1 hour).
  • You can remove the permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where your data is stored

Your details and resume are stored in our Google Drive and Google Sheets account, which only we can access.

Your choices

  • You can update your profile and resume at any time in My account.
  • To delete your account and data, email us and we will remove it.
